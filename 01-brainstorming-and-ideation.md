# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project

**LegalEase - AI-Powered Legal Document Generator**

## Problem statement

People and small organizations often need a first draft of a common agreement, but may not know how to structure one or where to start. LegalEase aims to turn a short set of user-provided details into an editable, clearly organized draft that can be reviewed before use.

## Idea and value

LegalEase combines a guided form, optional generative AI, an editable preview, and common document exports. The app covers non-disclosure agreements, employment agreements, residential leases, freelance services agreements, consulting agreements, and custom document types.

## Intended users

- Founders and small businesses preparing an initial agreement draft.
- Freelancers and clients discussing project terms.
- Landlords and tenants organizing proposed lease details.
- Individuals who need a structured starting point for a document.

## Initial scenarios

1. A founder supplies employer and employee details and key employment terms.
2. A freelancer and client define the scope and confidentiality terms for an NDA.
3. A landlord and tenant enter property and lease terms for a residential lease.

## Ideation decisions

- Keep the first release focused on drafting, editing, and exporting.
- Accept document type, parties and roles, key terms, effective date, jurisdiction, and language.
- Provide a local template mode so the app remains usable without an AI key.
- Present generated content as a draft and clearly state that it is not legal advice.

## Out of scope for the initial release

Legal review, enforceability checks, legal research, e-signatures, user accounts, persistent document storage, and public multi-tenant deployment are not implemented.

## Phase outcome

The idea is narrowed to an accessible document drafting studio with a human review step and editable exports. This concept is the basis for the requirements in [Phase 2](02-requirement-analysis.md).
